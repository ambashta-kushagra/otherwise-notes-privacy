Privacy Policy: Otherwise Notes

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Otherwise Notes is a notes app by Otherwise. It keeps everything on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics, and it does not ask for internet access. We never receive your notes, your PIN, or any other information about you.

Where your notes live

Your notes, folders, canvas stickies and settings are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. App backups are switched off, so they are not copied to a cloud backup either.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications: to show reminders and to tell you when a time capsule opens.
  • Fingerprint / biometrics: to unlock the app or your Private notes, if you turn those on. Your fingerprint is handled by Android and is never seen or stored by the app.
  • Run after restart: so your reminders still arrive after your phone restarts.

Your PIN

If you set a PIN, it is stored on your phone only, in a scrambled (hashed) form. The PIN stops other people from opening the app. It does not encrypt your notes on the phone.

Home-screen widget

If you add the widget, it shows the title and a short preview of your latest note on your home screen. It never shows private, sealed or deleted notes, and it shows nothing when app lock is on.

Exporting

If you export your notes as a text or PDF file, you choose where the file is saved. After that, the file is yours and is outside the app.

Deleting your data

Delete a note inside the app, or uninstall the app, and the data is removed from your phone.

Children

The app does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same address with a new date.
